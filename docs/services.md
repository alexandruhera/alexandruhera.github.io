# Services

I take on a limited number of consulting engagements alongside my day-to-day
work leading security operations. If your organization needs senior security
operations expertise without a full-time hire, this is for you.

## SOC advisory

Independent review of your security operations: detection coverage, triage
quality, escalation paths, metrics, and tooling. You get a concrete, prioritized
improvement plan — not a slideware maturity model.

## Detection engineering

Design and tuning of detections across endpoint, identity, and cloud.
Rule quality review, alert-fatigue reduction, and coverage mapping against
real-world attack techniques.

## Incident readiness

Response plans, playbooks, and tabletop exercises that reflect how incidents
actually unfold. When something does happen, a clear head start beats an
expensive panic.

## Security automation

Automating the repetitive layers of security operations — enrichment, triage,
reporting — including pragmatic use of AI agents where they genuinely help.
