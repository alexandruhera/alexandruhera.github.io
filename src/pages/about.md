# About

I'm Alexandru — a security operations leader based in Bucharest, Romania.

I currently manage a Security Operations Center delivering managed detection and
response, overseeing everything from log collection and threat detection to
incident triage, response, reporting, and customer engagement. Before that I
spent five years in hands-on incident response and digital forensics for a
global financial services company, worked as a senior SOC analyst at a major
MDR provider, and started out monitoring critical banking infrastructure.

Along the way I picked up a habit of automating the boring parts: response
actions, workflows, playbooks — and more recently, agentic AI systems that
handle the repetitive layers of security operations so humans can focus on
judgment calls.
